About

Who I am

I'm a technology enthusiast who has spent the last decade making things run — kitchens, embroidery production lines, a photography studio, and now the IT backbone of a trading office. That route taught me something most support engineers learn late: the hard part is rarely the hardware. It's the person standing next to it, having a bad day.

The path here wasn't standard. A chef's certificate in Durban. An operations role in Cape Town where I brought the latest technology into a creative business. A move to Vienna in 2019, a night-shift warehouse job at UPS that funded a full-time retraining into software and data — Harvard's CS50x, six months of full-time Java, Python, Google Data Analytics. Since 2024 I've been Trayport's sole IT presence in Vienna: first line to final fix.

I'm as good with humans as with hardware, and I bring the same energy to both.

"Empowering solutions through technology."
