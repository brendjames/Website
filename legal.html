Privacy Policy

Datenschutzerklärung gemäß Verordnung (EU) 2016/679 (DSGVO / GDPR) und DSG

Controller (Verantwortlicher): Brendon James Kirk, Vienna, Austria — brendonkirk86@gmail.com

This website is designed to be as data-minimal as possible. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no social media plugins. All fonts, scripts, and images are served from this website's own domain — visiting a page here triggers no requests to third-party servers.

1. Hosting & server log files

When you visit this site, the hosting provider automatically processes technical connection data (IP address, date and time of the request, requested URL, browser type) in server log files. This is technically necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its security and stability.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure operation of the website)
  • Retention: log files are retained only as long as the hosting provider requires for security purposes

2. Contact form

If you use the contact form, the data you enter (name, email address, message) is transmitted via the form-relay service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and delivered to my email inbox. The data is used solely to answer your enquiry and is not stored on this website. See the FormSubmit privacy policy for their handling of transmitted data. If you prefer not to use the form, you can email me directly instead.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract, e.g. job enquiries) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries)
  • Retention: emails are kept as long as needed to handle the enquiry, then deleted

3. Local storage (Arcade & Lab)

The Arcade and Lab pages store game high scores and display settings in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to me or anyone else, and can be deleted at any time via your browser settings. This storage is strictly necessary for the functions you request (saving your own scores and settings) and therefore requires no consent under § 165(3) TKG 2021.

4. Arcade global leaderboard (optional)

The Arcade offers an optional global top-10 leaderboard. It is used only if you actively choose to submit a score by entering initials after a game. When you do, the following is stored: the initials you type (1–3 characters, freely chosen — they need not identify you), your score, the game, and a timestamp. No name, email, account, or other personal data is required or requested.

This data is stored in a database on this website's own hosting in Austria (helloly.at) — it is not sent to any third party, and is never used for tracking or advertising. Submitting is entirely voluntary; if you don't enter initials, nothing is sent.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent, given by voluntarily submitting a score) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in offering a leaderboard feature)
  • Retention: only the top 50 scores per game are kept; lower scores are automatically removed. To have an entry deleted, email me with the initials and score.

5. Fonts

All web fonts are self-hosted on this website's own domain. No connection to Google Fonts or any other third-party font service takes place.

6. External links

This site links to external websites (e.g. GitHub). I have no influence over their content or data processing; the respective operators' privacy policies apply there.
