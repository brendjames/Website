Privacy Policy

Datenschutzerklärung gemäß Verordnung (EU) 2016/679 (DSGVO / GDPR) und DSG

Controller (Verantwortlicher): Brendon James Kirk, Vienna, Austria — brendonkirk86@gmail.com

This website is designed to be as data-minimal as possible. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no social media plugins. All fonts, scripts, and images are served from this website's own domain through its host (see section 1) — pages here load nothing from any other server.

1. Hosting (GitHub Pages) & server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit a page, GitHub automatically processes technical connection data (in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested URL and your browser type), and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This is technically necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its security and stability. I have no access to these logs.

This data may be processed in the USA. GitHub has certified its adherence to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). Details: GitHub's privacy statement.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and reliable delivery of the website)
  • Retention: determined by GitHub for security purposes

2. Contacting me by email

This site has no contact form. If you email me, I receive your email address, any name you include, and your message, and use them solely to answer your enquiry. My mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), so your email is processed and stored by Google as my email provider.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract, e.g. job enquiries) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries)
  • Retention: emails are kept as long as needed to handle the enquiry, then deleted

3. Local storage (Arcade & Lab)

The Arcade and Lab pages store game high scores and display settings in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to me or anyone else, and can be deleted at any time via your browser settings. This storage is strictly necessary for the functions you request (saving your own scores and settings) and therefore requires no consent under § 165(3) TKG 2021.

4. Fonts

All web fonts are self-hosted on this website's own domain. No connection to Google Fonts or any other third-party font service takes place.

5. External links

This site links to external websites (e.g. GitHub). I have no influence over their content or data processing; the respective operators' privacy policies apply there.
